ACCOUNT: GUEST
PASSPHRASE: GUEST

DR. FALKEN'S PRIMARY ADMINISTRATIVE ACCOUNT MAINTAINS THIS
SAME CONVENTION FOR SIMPLICITY DURING THE DEVELOPMENT PHASE.
