TO: ALL FACULTY
FROM: PRINCIPAL
DATE: SEPTEMBER 12, 1983

SUBJECT: UNAUTHORIZED SYSTEM ACCESS

IT HAS COME TO MY ATTENTION THAT STUDENTS ARE ATTEMPTING TO ACCESS
RESTRICTED AREAS OF THE DATANET. PLEASE ENSURE ALL TERMINALS ARE
SECURED AFTER CLASS.

ANY STUDENT CAUGHT TAMPERING WITH GRADES OR THE SYSTEM FILES WILL
FACE IMMEDIATE SUSPENSION.
